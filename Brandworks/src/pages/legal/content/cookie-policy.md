## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies work in much the same way, such as local storage, which keeps information in your browser, and pixels, which are tiny pieces of code that tell a service a page has loaded. In this policy, "cookies" covers all of them.

Some cookies are set by us (first-party cookies). Others are set by the services we use, such as analytics or advertising platforms (third-party cookies).
