## How we protect your data

We use HTTPS across the site and two-factor authentication on our accounts. Only team members who need personal data for their work can access it, and we choose service providers with strong security records.

No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we'll tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires, explain what happened and tell you what we're doing about it.

## Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, you can:

- ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you, what we do with it and who we've shared it with
- ask us to correct, complete or update it
- ask us to erase it, unless the law requires us to keep it
- withdraw any consent you've given
- nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or become unable to use them yourself
- raise a grievance with us and, if we don't resolve it, complain to the Data Protection Board of India

To use any of these rights, email [{{company.privacyEmail}}](<mailto:{{company.privacyEmail}}>) with "Privacy request" in the subject line. We may ask you to confirm your identity before we act. We'll respond within 30 days.
