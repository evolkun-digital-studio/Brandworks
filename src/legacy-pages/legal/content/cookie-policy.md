## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies work in much the same way, such as local storage, which keeps information in your browser, and pixels, which are tiny pieces of code that tell a service a page has loaded. In this policy, "cookies" covers all of them.

Some cookies are set by us (first-party cookies). Others are set by the services we use, such as analytics or advertising platforms (third-party cookies).

## How we ask for your consent

{{#if optionalCookies}}When you first visit, a banner asks for your choice before any optional cookie is set. It is designed to be fair, in line with the Central Consumer Protection Authority's Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns, 2023:

- **Accept all** and **Reject all** are the same size and equally easy to find
- nothing is pre-ticked, and until you choose, no optional cookie is set
- you can choose group by group in **Cookie settings**
- the banner never blocks the content or nags you to change your mind

We remember your choice in a cookie for 6 months, then ask again. We'll also ask again if we add a new tool or change how we use cookies.
