## How we protect your data

We follow the reasonable security safeguards set out in Rule 6 of the DPDP Rules, including:

- **encryption:** HTTPS across the whole site, and encrypted storage with our providers
- **access control:** two-factor authentication on our accounts, and access only for team members who need the data for their work
- **logging and monitoring:** we keep access and security logs for at least one year so we can detect and investigate misuse
- **backups:** so data can be restored if it is lost or corrupted
- **contracts:** every service provider must keep data secure on our behalf

## If there's a data breach

No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will:

- tell you without delay, in plain language, what happened, what it may mean for you, what we're doing about it and what you can do to protect yourself
- report it to the Data Protection Board of India without delay, and send the Board a full report within 72 hours
- report cyber security incidents to CERT-In within 6 hours, as its directions require

## Your rights

Under the DPDP Act and DPDP Rules, you can:

- **Access:** ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you, what we do with it, and who we've shared it with
- **Correction:** ask us to correct, complete or update it
- **Erasure:** ask us to erase it, unless the law requires us to keep it
- **Withdraw consent:** at any time, as described above
- **Nominate:** name someone to use these rights for you if you die or become unable to use them yourself
- **Grievance redressal:** raise a complaint with us and get a response

To use any of these rights, email [{{company.privacyEmail}}](<mailto:{{company.privacyEmail}}>) with "Privacy request" in the subject line, and tell us which right you want to use. We may ask you to confirm your identity first, using only the details we already hold or the minimum needed. We aim to respond within 30 days, and always within the 90 days the DPDP Rules allow.

The DPDP Act also asks Data Principals not to give false information or file false or frivolous complaints.
